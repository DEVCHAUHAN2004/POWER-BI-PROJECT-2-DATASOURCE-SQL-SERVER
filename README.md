📌 About the Project

Men's Fashion Brand Analytics is an interactive Power BI Business
Intelligence project designed to analyze and compare the performance
of fashion brands.

The dashboard converts brand-level data into actionable business
insights and provides a clear view of:

💸 Discount strategy

📈 Profitability

👕 Product variety

💰 Average sales price

🏆 Top-performing brands

⚠️ Lower-performing brands

The report follows a premium dark navy + beige/gold fashion theme
for a professional executive-dashboard experience.

🎯 Business Questions

This dashboard answers important business questions such as:

Which brands offer the highest average discounts?

Which brands have the highest average profit percentage?

Which brands have the widest product variety?

Which brands have the highest average sales price?

Which brands appear among the lower-profit brands?

How do different brands compare across major business metrics?

🧠 Key Business Insights

1️⃣ Discount Strategy

Some brands operate with extremely high average discounts, with iVogue
reaching 89% in the displayed analysis.

2️⃣ Profitability

SHOWOFF and Valen Club show the highest displayed average profit
percentage at 17%.

3️⃣ Product Assortment

The Indian Garage Co has the largest displayed assortment with 51
varieties, indicating a broader product range.

4️⃣ Premium Pricing

ARMANI EXCHANGE has the highest displayed average sales price at
approximately 6.1K, suggesting a premium pricing position.

5️⃣ Brand Comparison

The dashboard allows stakeholders to compare brands from multiple
perspectives instead of relying on a single KPI.
